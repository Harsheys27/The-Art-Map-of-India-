# 🇮🇳 The Art Map of India

**The Art Map of India** is an interactive digital museum and educational archive built to explore, preserve, and showcase the rich artistic heritage of the Indian subcontinent.

From **Madhubani in Bihar to Chola bronzes in Tamil Nadu**, the platform connects India's regions, traditions, artists, and art forms through an immersive georeferenced map.

### ✦ Highlights

- 🗺️ **18+ Regions** — Explore India's diverse artistic landscapes
- 🎨 **7+ Art Categories** — Discover folk, tribal, classical, contemporary, and more
- 🏛️ **30+ Traditions** — From ancient practices to living cultural traditions
- ⏳ **5,000+ Years of Heritage** — Trace India's artistic evolution across time
- 👨‍🎨 **Artist & Tradition Profiles** — Learn about the people and communities behind the art
- 🔎 **Interactive Exploration** — Filter and discover art based on region, category, and tradition
- 📚 **Educational Archive** — A structured resource for learning about Indian cultural heritage

> **Preserving India's artistic memory, one region at a time.**
